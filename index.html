Powerful Features

Everything you need for a blooming house

Designed to simplify plant ownership. We combine smart hardware data modeling with intuitive software features to keep your plants happy.

Smart Watering Schedule

Never forget to water or accidentally overwater again. Bloom dynamically adjusts reminders based on local temperature, weather, and specific species requirements.

AI Leaf Diagnostic Scan

Yellow spots? Wilting leaves? Snap a quick photo with the Bloom scanner to identify over 1,200 plant diseases instantly and get structured treatment solutions.

Customizable Calendars

Consolidate watering, fertilizing, misting, and repotting routines in a unified care feed. Sync it directly with your Google or Apple Calendar easily.

Growth Milestones Tracker

Create beautiful visual growth logs of your plants. Document new leaves, flowers, or repotting phases to generate beautiful, exportable time-lapse stories.
